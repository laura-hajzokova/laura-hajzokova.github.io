About

A third-year PhD student with strong mathematical background focusing on fundamental problems in interpretable dimensionality reduction and representation learning for single-cell applications. A central goal of my work is preserving the topology and geometry of data in lower-dimensional representations, as well as interpreting and analysing the latent space.

  • Representation learning
  • Dimensionality reduction
  • Geometric & topological deep learning
  • Single-cell omics
